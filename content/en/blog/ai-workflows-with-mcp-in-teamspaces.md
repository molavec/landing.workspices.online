Artificial intelligence has evolved from simple standalone chatbots into collaborative agents that interact with your toolstack. However, an AI agent is only as good as the context it can access.

The **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** bridges this gap, enabling your favorite AI models to understand and interact with your Teamspaces directly.

## What is Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

The Model Context Protocol is an open standard designed to standardize how AI applications communicate with local and remote data sources. Instead of writing custom API bridges for every AI tool:

- AI assistants can safely query your workspace structures.
- Agents can fetch relevant Quicklinks and tasks relevant to user queries.
- Workflows stay consistent across tools like Claude Desktop, Cursor, and Antigravity.

## How MCP Integrates with Teamspaces

By enabling MCP integration in Teamspaces:

1. **Context-Aware Assistance:** When you ask your AI assistant to review project status, it reads the active Quicklinks and contextual tasks without manual copy-pasting.
2. **Automated Resource Linking:** Generate new Quicklinks and assign contextual tasks through conversational prompts.
3. **Multi-Agent Collaboration:** Different specialized AI assistants can coordinate across the same unified workspace data model.

## The Future of Collaborative Work

As AI teammates become standard in modern engineering and agency teams, having an organized foundation like Teamspaces ensures both humans and AI models stay aligned on the exact same sources of truth.
